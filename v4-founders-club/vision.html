Our Vision

Venture Forge Austin exists to fuel the growth of the Kingdom economy by investing in transformative, faith-driven entrepreneurship.

Forged, not launched

Great ventures aren't simply launched — they're forged. Like a blacksmith shapes raw steel through heat, pressure, and skilled craftsmanship, Venture Forge brings together entrepreneurs, mentors, investors, and a community of faith to refine bold ideas into businesses that create lasting value.

Two days here, once a year,can reshape the trajectory of the next decade.

We believe

We believe work is a God-given calling, and that when the sparks of innovation meet the fire of generosity, wisdom, and investment, ventures are forged that transform lives, strengthen communities, and advance the common good.

Our mission

“Venture Forge exists to unite and mobilize Austin's faith-driven ecosystem by accelerating Kingdom-minded ventures, equipping founders, connecting capital with calling, and celebrating innovative solutions that advance human flourishing and the Kingdom of God.”

Each year

Our vision is to be Austin's go-to hub where faith-driven capital, ventures, and leaders meet and collaborate for Kingdom impact. Each year, we gather investors, entrepreneurs, and Kingdom-minded leaders for two days of candid conversation, shared conviction, and real collaboration. The goal is not spectacle; it is partnership.
