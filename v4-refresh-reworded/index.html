Our Vision

Fueling the Kingdom Economy

We believe work is a God-given calling, and that when the sparks of innovation meet the fire of generosity, wisdom, and investment, ventures are forged that transform lives.

Each year, we gather investors, entrepreneurs, and Kingdom-minded leaders for two days of conversation, shared conviction, and real collaboration. The goal is not spectacle; it's partnership.

Austin — a city of builders, dreamers, and doers — is our home. Two days here, once a year, can reshape the trajectory of the next decade.
